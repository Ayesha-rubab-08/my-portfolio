AI Customer Support Assistant

An n8n workflow that reads each customer message, works out what it is about and how urgent it is, and drafts a reply from an FAQ.

  • Gemini sorts every message by category, sentiment, urgency and confidence, and replies are drafted from the FAQ with RAG.
  • Urgent messages trigger a Gmail alert, and low-confidence ones are flagged for a person to review.
  • Tested the urgency prompt on 25 messages I wrote myself across 3 versions, reaching 25/25 on the final one, with retries and a separate error-alert workflow.

  • Gemini API
  • Google Sheets
  • Gmail
